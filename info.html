I'm a creative technologist and behavioural researcher with 10+ years of UX and design research experience. I build NLP pipelines, AI-powered workflows, and data tools — with a particular interest in how personalisation systems model and respond to human signals.

I care about the quiet ways platforms, maps, and services shape what people see, choose, and feel. Language is the signal I follow. The vocabulary someone uses to review a supermarket, the framing a news outlet chooses for a conflict, the reply pattern of a bot vs. a human in a debate thread. These are all legible if you read them structurally. Sometimes I make the patterns visible through interactive tools or installations that put language back into physical space.

I'm currently a research assistant at Columbia, working on climate data UX for the IPCC Data Distribution Centre. Before this, I was lead UX researcher and experience designer at Kantar Public Singapore, design researcher at Frog Inc., and co-founded a digital fabrication makerspace at the ArtScience Museum and a STEM education platform.

I recently completed my MA in Interactive Media Arts at NYU Tisch ITP/IMA. I'm now completing an MA in Computational Social Science at Columbia.
