About

I'm a behavioural researcher and creative technologist. I build NLP pipelines, AI-powered workflows, and data tools — and make things with them.

My work sits at the intersection of social science and technology. I'm interested in the language people use to describe the world — and what that language reveals about community, care, class, and belief. I've built systems that score media framing across 77 news sources, mapped vocabulary diversity across 480+ NYC supermarkets, and made words drift through physical space in response to their semantic gravity.

Before this, I was lead UX researcher at Kantar Public Singapore, designer at Frog Inc., and co-founded a digital fabrication makerspace at the ArtScience Museum and a STEM education platform. I'm currently completing an MA in Quantitative Methods of Social Sciences at Columbia.

For further insights into my work, you may download my CV. Or, reach out to say hello.
