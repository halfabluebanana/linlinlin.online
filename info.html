projects about writings

I study how AI systems are designed to behave, and what that does to the people and communities they interact with.

Language is the signal I follow. The vocabulary someone uses to review a supermarket, the framing a news outlet chooses for a conflict, the reply pattern of a bot vs. a human in a debate thread. These patterns are legible if you read them structurally. My work builds tools and research that make them visible, sometimes as data pipelines and interactive applications, sometimes as installations that put language back into physical space.

I'm interested in the behavioural mechanics of AI at scale: how model behaviour design, personalisation architecture, and interface choices propagate into interaction patterns, community dynamics, and trust, and what it means to design AI systems that are genuinely oriented towards the people using them, not just towards engagement.

Recent projects include network analysis of bot vs. human interaction structure across Reddit communities, LLM-powered media framing analysis across 53,000 articles and 77 news sources, and behavioural studies of linguistic asymmetry across New York supermarket reviews.

Before this: lead behavioural researcher at Kantar Public Singapore, design researcher at Frog, co-founder of FabCafe at ArtScience Museum and Saturday Kids.

MA Quantitative Methods of Social Sciences, Columbia University. MA Interactive Media Arts, NYU Tisch ITP/IMA.
