word soup

Language as a living visual system. Word Soup combines natural language processing with cellular automata — a system where language becomes the seed for emergent visual behaviour. Words are parsed, weighted, and fed into a grid of cells that evolve according to rules, producing patterns that feel alive without being predictable. No two runs are the same.

Python: NLP pipeline — text parsing, tokenisation, semantic weighting of words, assignment of cell states.

JavaScript: cellular automata engine + canvas rendering. Each cell's next state is determined by its neighbours and its inherited semantic weight from the source text.

Screened at B-Dome, Berlin. December 2025.
