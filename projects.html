Quantitative researcher finishing an MA in Quantitative Methods of the Social Sciences at Columbia, December 2026. I design experiments and build data pipelines to answer one kind of question: how AI systems actually behave, and what they change about the people using them. Before Columbia I was lead experience designer in the behavioural change and experience design team at Kantar Public Singapore, on mixed-methods studies up to n=9,000 for national government clients.
